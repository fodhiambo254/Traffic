01 / THE CHALLENGE

Getting around
shouldn’t be the hard part.

At Kayole Junction, everyday trips are made harder by infrastructure and traffic-control gaps. These are practical issues, and they deserve practical solutions.

01
↗

Roads in poor condition

Damaged and inadequate roads slow movement and make journeys less safe for drivers, pedestrians and passengers.

02
✳

Too few traffic officers

Without enough officers at busy points, congestion and confusion can build up during peak travel times.

03
◷

Unmanaged travel times

Uncoordinated traffic flow means delays are difficult to predict and daily commutes take longer than they should.

02 / A BETTER WAY FORWARD

Three changes.
One smoother journey.

Invest in the road network, put trained officers where they’re needed, and connect Kayole to rail. Together, these steps can take pressure off the junction.

01 INFRASTRUCTURE

Build and improve
the roads.

Repair damaged sections and improve key routes around the junction. Safer, better-connected roads help people and goods move with fewer delays.
